I am a researcher specializing in Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), multicriteria decision analysis, and stakeholder network modeling, with a particular interest in the environmental transition of energy systems.

I obtained my PhD in Industrial Engineering from **CentraleSupélec, Université Paris-Saclay**, in October 2026. My doctoral research was conducted at the Laboratoire Génie Industriel (LGI), in collaboration with IKOS Consulting.

My research focuses on two complementary areas:

- **Sustainability assessment and decision support:** I developed a parametric and prospective LCA framework coupled with multicriteria decision analysis to compare energy systems regional railway transport. My work aims to model futur systems and their uncertainty to support decision makers.

- **Stakeholder network modeling:** I developed a methodology to reduce risks in public-private interactions for public decision-making. Design Structure Matrix, indicators and clustering algorithms are used to reorganize stakeholders interventions.

Before my PhD, I graduated as an engineer from **ENGEES Strasbourg**, specializing in environmental and hydraulic structure and modeling engineering.

My broader research interests include environmental assessment, eco-design, sustainable transportation, computational methods, and decision-support methodologies.
